# Dense-Mem

Dense-Mem retains source memory and organizes it for later retrieval.

## Language

**Evidence**:
Exact submitted source content with provenance and a governed lifecycle.
_Avoid_: Extracted fact, generated summary

**Occurrence**:
A particular submission of Evidence, retaining its source and authorship even
when equivalent content is shared.

**Factual Relationship**:
A subject-predicate-object claim connecting an Entity to another Entity or a
typed Value, with supporting Evidence. This differs from topic membership.
_Avoid_: Using Relationship to mean any association

**Topic**:
A named organizational concept used to discover related Evidence.

**Topic membership**:
A derived association placing Evidence under a Topic. Membership describes
organization and does not assert that one item supports another item's claims.
